About RSJ

Located inside Asia’s largest integrated news centre, RSJ is powered by the editorial engine of Republic Media Network - home to India’s most watched news channels and the heart of fearless journalism.

At RSJ, the newsroom is your classroom, and your mentors are the journalists who shape national narratives every day.

We don’t believe journalism can be taught through textbooks alone. At RSJ, you learn by doing — on the field, in the TV & Digital newsrooms, inside the studio, and at the heart of breaking news. Just practical hands-on experience.

From day one, you’re immersed in LIVE LEARNING. If you’re bold enough to chase the truth, brave enough to be on the frontlines, and sharp enough to lead the next generation of news — this is where you begin. OUR CAMPUS IS YOUR CLASSROOM.

RSJ Diploma Programme

About this programme

Future Ready Modules

Master the art of creating impactful, engaging digital content tailored for social media, websites, and mobile-first platforms. Learn to blend storytelling with format-specific design to drive viewer interaction. LEARN LIVE in our digital newsroom.

Develop skills to write concise, informative, and SEO-optimized articles for digital platforms. Understand how to write headlines, push notifications, and real-time updates for fast-paced online news. LEARN LIVE in our digital newsroom.

Learn the end-to-end process of video production for digital—from scripting and shooting to editing and publishing. Work with mobile journalism tools and software used across online newsrooms. LEARN LIVE in our digital newsroom.

Understand how AI is transforming journalism, and train in using AI tools for research, content, transcription, and editing. Learn advanced fact-checking techniques to fight misinformation and uphold journalistic integrity. LEARN LIVE in our broadcast & Digital newsrooms.

Get trained to handle high-pressure situations during breaking news. Learn how to verify information, coordinate with teams, and push content across platforms in real time. LEARN LIVE on our News Gathering Desk.

Understand the grammar and ethics of live and breaking news. Learn editorial decision-making, real-time scripting, and the responsibilities that come with going on-air without a script. LEARN LIVE on the Breaking News Desk.

Learn how to anchor & report from the studios & field for live TV and digital channels. This includes structuring a live report, preparing standups and telling a story in a Breaking News situation while being factual and responsible. LEARN LIVE in the studios, on the Field & in our Control Rooms.

Get trained in identifying newsworthy stories, building source networks, and gathering inputs across beats like Politics, Sports, Business, Crime, and Civic Affairs. Learn how news is gathered, factchecked and transmitted for both TV & Digital. LEARN LIVE on the News Gathering Desk.

Hone your skills in writing, anchoring, and presenting for English television. Learn what goes into running a 24x7 news engine. LEARN LIVE in the Republic TV Newsroom.

Train in Hindi news writing and working on the Hindi News Desk. Train to present for both national and regional viewers. Understand the cultural nuances that makes a successful Hindi News Channel. LEARN LIVE in the Republic Bharat Newsroom.

Master the art of crisp, impactful writing for TV bulletins and video edits. Learn to write intros, anchor cues, and tickers. LEARN LIVE in the Republic TV & Republic Bharat Newsrooms.

Understand how rundown software works and learn to manage bulletins minute by minute. As the news breaks. Experience the editor’s role in sequencing stories, coordinating feeds, and dealing with Breaking Story. LEARN LIVE on the Desk.

Train in managing a live studio floor—coordinating camera cuts, live inserts, panel switching, and timing during a rolling news broadcast. LEARN LIVE in the Republic TV Control Room

Learn camera handling, lighting, and studio setup for both television and digital platforms. Train on multi-cam studio shoots and ENG (Electronic News Gathering) kits. LEARN LIVE in the Republic Production Control Rooms.

Understand the laws governing media and journalism in India. From defamation and contempt to copyright and RTI, learn the legal do’s and don’ts of reporting. LEARN LIVE with our Editors equipped with the law.

Get exposure to the backend systems of a live newsroom—automation systems, teleprompters, MCR (Master Control Room), and newsroom software that drive daily operations. LEARN LIVE on Campus.

Learn how to use industry-standard editing software like Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and newsroom-integration tools to edit clips quickly for TV and digital release. LEARN LIVE in our Newsrooms with Editors.

Go beyond the scores. Learn to cover live matches, locker-room controversies, sporting scandals, and athlete profiles. At RSJ, you’ll be trained to report from stadiums, and live broadcast centres — mastering the art of writing match reports, conducting interviews, giving Live updates and breaking sports news across TV and digital platforms. LEARN LIVE with R.Sport Fit teams.

Cover the nation's frontlines with integrity and depth. RSJ trains you to report on defence, national security, military operations, and strategic affairs with a NATION FIRST approach. Learn how to track defence policy, attend briefings, cover crises, and understand the sensitivities of reporting from the corridors of power to the borders of the country. LEARN LIVE from our ace Defence editors.

Decode markets, analyze budgets, and break financial stories that move economies. At RSJ, you will learn to simplify complex financial information for a mass audience — covering corporate news, stock markets, economic policies, start-ups, and business leaders, both for TV and digital newsrooms. LEARN LIVE with our Republic Business Team.

Understand satellite technology, the workflow of news feeds, and how broadcast signals are managed. Get familiar with news uplinking/downlinking and content relay systems. LEARN LIVE with our Technical Leads.
