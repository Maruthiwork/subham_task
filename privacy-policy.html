PRIVACY POLICY


Effective Date: April 15, 2025

Republic School of Journalism (RSJ), a division of Republic Media Network, is committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors, applicants, and users of this website. This policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information.


  • Information We Collect

    We may collect the following types of personal information:
    • Name, contact number, email address, city, and academic details
    • Information submitted through online forms, applications, and enquiries

  • How We Use Your Information

    The information you provide is used to:
    • Process applications and communicate admission-related updates
    • Respond to queries or requests for information
    • Improve the website experience and monitor performance
    • Share updates about RSJ courses, events, and deadlines (only with consent)

  • Data Security

    We take reasonable and industry-standard measures to protect the confidentiality and security of your personal data. However, RSJ cannot guarantee absolute security over the internet.

  • Data Sharing

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your information may only be shared with:
    • Internal academic or administrative teams at Republic Media Network
    • External partners assisting with secure payment gateways or website analytics (where applicable)
    • Authorities, if required under law

  • Cookies & Tracking

    The RSJ website may use cookies or analytics tools to enhance functionality and track user engagement. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, although some site features may not function properly.

  • Third-Party Links

    Our website may contain links to third-party websites. RSJ is not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage users to review their policies separately.

  • Policy Updates

    RSJ may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. All updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

  • Contact Us
